Nothing accumulates between frames. A scene at t = 8.2s is identical every time you visit it — from the scrubber, from a replay, from the audience window, or after a browser crash mid-talk.

  1. →Scrubbing is exact. Drag to any instant of any scene. The frame you land on is the frame the audience would have seen.
  2. →The audience window is a mirror, not a guess. State syncs over postMessage with a per-session token. Notes stay on your side of the glass.
  3. →Reduced motion is the final frame. Motion off, prefers-reduced-motion, or a preview thumbnail — all render the scene's resolved end state, by construction.

Install

Register the server with your MCP client. Node 18+, no dependencies, nothing phones home.

02
Author

create_deck, then add_scene — your agent reads scene_reference and writes scenes as JSON. Real datasets go in through set_data, not hardcoded into slides.

build_deck emits one offline folder. Open index.html, press P, and share only the audience window.
